Fine-Tuned Universe

Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom. Orbits are simulated directly (140,000 stars in three interacting galaxies, plus 40 distant ones). Star brightness and lifetime follow the Eddington scaling (luminosity ∝ (G·μ)⁴). Big Bang helium and deuterium use the neutron freeze-out estimate with response factors from Meißner et al. 2023 and 2025 and Alvey et al. 2020; the quark-mass slopes are calibrated on the 2025 deuterium and helium bounds (the sign of the helium slope is assumed). The carbon and oxygen windows use the stellar limits quoted in the 2025 review: about ±7.5% for α and 0.4% to 5% for quark masses. “Life” is a simplified indicator: a star that lives over 4 billion years and makes carbon and oxygen. Whether the constants are fine-tuned is debated.
